# Activity 3 — AI Applications & Ethics Briefing
## Real Applications, Real Ethics, Real Law

**Course:** Introduction to Artificial Intelligence
**Sessions:** 4 and 5
**Topic:** AI Applications Across Industries; Ethical and Legal Aspects of AI

---

## Introduction

Session 4 surveyed how AI is actually being used across industries today — from radiology to agriculture to logistics. Session 5 examined the ethical and legal questions that use raises, in Mexico and internationally. This activity asks your team to bring both together: pick one real-world domain, describe the real AI application in it, and analyze the real ethical and legal implications of that application.

Unlike Activity 1's fictional client, everything here is grounded in the real world — real applications, real companies, real regulations (GDPR, Mexico's data-protection law, UN and international AI ethics work). Your deliverable is the same shape as Activity 1: a short **PowerPoint presentation** and a **video** (in English) presenting your findings.

---

## What You Will Present

| Required section | Session it draws on | What it must answer |
|---|---|---|
| The Real Application | Session 4 | What is the AI system actually doing, and how does it create value in this domain? |
| Ethical & Legal Analysis | Session 5 | What specific regulation, principle, or tension applies here — and why does it matter for this application? |
| Recommendation | Sessions 4–5 combined | On balance, should this application be encouraged, regulated more tightly, or approached with caution — and under what safeguards? |
| Team Reflection | Your own experience | What surprised you or changed your view while researching this |

## Academic Integrity

- Two teams may not present the same domain — coordinate early.
- You may research beyond the course session content, but the analysis and recommendation must be your team's own.
- Cite your sources on the Sources slide.

---

## Tips for Success

- **Before you build slides:** agree on your recommendation first, then build the application/ethics sections as the evidence supporting it.
- **While researching:** find one concrete real-world detail beyond what's already given in `A3_DomainOptions.md` — that's what separates a strong submission from a generic one.
- **While recording:** rehearse once before recording for real.
