# Activity 1 — AI Foundations Briefing
## An AI Readiness Briefing for Katún Manufactura

**Course:** Introduction to Artificial Intelligence
**Sessions:** 1, 2, and 3
**Topic:** AI Definitions, History, and Interdisciplinary Foundations

---

## Introduction

Your team has been hired as outside AI consultants by **Katún Manufactura**, a mid-size precision-parts manufacturer in Mérida, Yucatán. Its board is about to vote on a three-year, $185,000 contract with an AI vendor — and they've asked your team to independently brief them before they sign.

This activity has no code and no notebook. Instead, you'll apply what Sessions 1–3 taught you about what AI actually is, where it came from, and what it takes to make it work — to a single, concrete business decision. Your deliverable is a short **PowerPoint presentation** and a **video** (in English) walking Katún's board through your findings and your own reflection.

---

| Required section | Session it draws on | What it must answer |
|---|---|---|

| Interdisciplinary Foundations | Session 3 — Relation with other disciplines | What expertise, beyond the software purchase, would Katún actually need? |
| Recommendation & Synthesis | Sessions 1–3 combined | Sign, don't sign, or sign with conditions — and why |

## Getting Started

### Step 1 — Read the brief

Open `A1_CompanyBrief.md` and read it completely as a team. Every fact, quote, and number you need is already there.

### Step 2 — Plan your slides

Follow `A1_PresentationGuide.md` phase by phase to outline your deck before you start designing slides.

### Step 3 — Build your deck (8–10 slides)

Build your PowerPoint covering the required sections above. Keep it concise — 8–10 slides is a hard target, not a minimum to pad out.

### Step 4 — Record your video (5–7 minutes, in English)

Record a video in which your team walks through the deck and delivers your recommendation and reflection out loud. All team members must speak.

### Step 5 — Submit

See **Submission** below.

### Grading notes

- Both the slides and the video must be in English.
- Content that could apply to "any AI vendor pitch" instead of Katún Manufactura specifically will not receive full credit under Sections 1–3 — see `A1_PresentationGuide.md`'s Common Problems table.

## Academic Integrity

- The company, vendor, and board quotes in `A1_CompanyBrief.md` are fictional and must be used exactly as written so that every team is graded against the same facts.
- You may research real AI history to support your Historical Lesson section, but the analysis and recommendation must be your team's own.
- Cite any outside sources you use on your Sources slide.

---

## Tips for Success

- **Before you build slides:** agree as a team on your recommendation first — it's much easier to build Sections 1–3 as supporting arguments once you know where you're headed.
- **While designing slides:** one idea per slide. If a slide needs more than a few bullet points to explain, it's probably two slides.
- **While recording:** do one full rehearsal run-through before recording for real — it's the fastest way to fix awkward handoffs between speakers.
